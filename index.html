FAQ

How should I wash my underwear?

Machine wash at 30°C with similar colours, then line dry. Skip fabric softener — it breaks down the elastic faster.

How often should I replace underwear?

A good rule of thumb is every 6–12 months, or sooner if the waistband has lost its stretch or the fabric has thinned.

Can I return underwear?

For hygiene reasons we accept returns on unopened packs within 30 days. If something's faulty, get in touch and we'll sort it out.
