Good to know

  • Each cookie weighs about 80 g and is about 9 cm across.
  • They keep 4 to 5 days in a closed tin. Warm one for 5 minutes at 150 °C and it tastes fresh from the oven again.
  • Everything is baked in a home kitchen that also handles nuts, gluten, egg, milk and soy. Have an allergy? Mention it in your order.
